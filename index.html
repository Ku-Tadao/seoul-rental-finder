Seoul Rental Value Finder

Value = (rent + maintenance + deposit × ÷ 12) ÷ usable m² × (1 + × age ÷ 10). Lower is better.How this works

You get a deposit back, so only the cost of having that money locked up counts: loan interest or lost savings. The default rate is what the listings themselves imply when jeonse and monthly offers exist in the same complex at the same size. Set it to your own rate under Formula settings.

Old buildings bring problems the rent doesn't show, so age makes them earn their place. At the default, a 30-year-old building counts as 1.9× as expensive per m² as a new one, and anything older is hidden. Both are preferences, not measurements, so set them to taste. Age is years since the building's official approval date (사용승인일).
